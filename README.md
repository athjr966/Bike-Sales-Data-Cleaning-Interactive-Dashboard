# Bike-Sales-Data-Cleaning-Interactive-Dashboard

## Objective: 
To transform raw sales data into a clean, structured dataset and analyse customer purchasing behaviour, demographic trends, and sales patterns using Microsoft Excel.

## Overview:
This project focuses on cleaning and analysing raw sales data to make it usable for reporting. 
The raw dataset contained duplicates and inconsistent formatting.

## Project Workflow

## 1. Data Cleaning Process:
- Removed duplicate records
- Standardised formats (e.g., text)
- Cleaned text data

## 2. Analysis & Dashboard:
- Used Pivot Tables to summarize sales data
- Calculated average income per purchase
- Analysed the relationship between customer commute distance and purchasing behaviour
- Segmented customers into age brackets to identify demographic trends
- Created charts to visualise key insights

## Key Questions 
## 1. What is the average income per purchase?
Average income per purchase for overall is $57,963
Average income per purchase for female is $55,774 meanwhile for male is $60,124.

## 2. Does customer commute distance relate to purchasing behaviour?
Yes, there seem to be a correlation. The closer the distance, the higher the purchase. 

## 3. Which age groups are more likely to purchase a bike?
The age group that most likely to purchase bikes were middle aged that ranges from 31 to 54 years old

## 4. What demographic patterns can be identified among customers?
- Customers whose age were at the range of 31–54 years old had a higher proportion of bike purchases
- Males were the most likely to purchase bikes regardless of their marital status. However, married women were more likely to purchase bikes.
- Married customers had higher proportion of bike purchases
- Males have higher incomes compared to women at $60,124 and had higher proportion of bike purchases
- Customers with bachelor degree had the highest purchase, followed by partial college, graduate degree, highschool and then partial highschool
 
## 5. What factors appear to distinguish purchasers from non-purchasers?
- Age, gender and distance.  seem to have drastic difference

## Key Findings 
Age: Customers aged 31–54 showed the highest proportion of bike purchases.
Gender: Male customers had a higher proportion of bike purchases overall, while married female customers showed a higher purchasing tendency among women.
Commute Distance: Customers with shorter commute distances showed higher bike purchase rates.
Income: Male purchasers had a higher average income ($60,124) compared with female purchasers ($55,774), with an overall average of $57,963.
Marital Status: Married customers showed a higher proportion of bike purchases compared with unmarried customers.
Education: Customers with a Bachelor's degree recorded the highest number/proportion of bike purchases among the education groups analysed.
